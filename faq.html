FAQ's

Frequently Asked Questions

Returns & Exchanges Policy

What is your policy if the item does not fit or meet expectations? Returns and refunds are accepted only for valid reasons such as damaged or incorrect items. Requests must be made within a specified time after delivery, and proof (photo or receipt) may be required.

Delivery Issues

What happens if the customer is not available to receive the package? If the recipient is unavailable, we will contact them or the sender. Redelivery may be scheduled, but additional delivery fees may apply.

Order Modification or Cancellation

Can I change or cancel my order after checkout? Orders may be modified or canceled within a limited time after placing the order. Once the order is processed or dispatched, changes may no longer be possible.

Same-day or Express Delivery

Do you offer same-day or rush delivery options? Yes, same-day delivery is available depending on flower availability, location, and order cutoff time. Please confirm before placing your order.

Product Accuracy

Will the flower arrangement look exactly like the picture shown? We strive to match the arrangement as closely as possible. Slight variations may occur due to seasonal flowers or availability.

Minimum Purchase Requirement

Is there a minimum order amount required to place an order? Yes, a minimum order value may apply depending on the arrangement and delivery location

Order Confirmation

How will I know if my order has been successfully placed and confirmed? You will receive a confirmation message or email once your order is successfully received and processed

Ordering Process

How do I place an order through your website, social media, or directly with the shop? Orders can be placed through our website, social media pages, or by directly messaging us with your order details.

Payment Methods

What payment options do you accept? We accept cash on delivery, bank transfer, and e-wallet payments. Available options will be shown during checkout.

Custom Orders

Can I request specific flowers or customize my arrangement? Yes, you can request specific flowers or customized arrangements depending on seasonal availability.

Scheduled Delivery

Can I schedule my flower delivery for a future date? Yes, you can select the delivery date and time when placing your order. Early booking is recommended

Personal Messages

Can I include a personal message with my flower arrangement? Yes, you can include a personalized message, which will be added to a card with your flowers.

Pickup Option

Can I pick up my order directly from the shop instead of having it delivered? Yes, pickup is available. Please schedule a pickup time so your arrangement is ready.

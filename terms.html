Pre-Order Period

Pre-orders for fresh flower arrangements are accepted within the announced ordering period prior to the delivery dates.

Early Payment Discount

All arrangements ordered and fully paid on or before the announced cut-off date will receive a 10% discount on fresh arrangements only.

A reminder will be sent before the cut-off date for balance settlement.

Cancellation Policy

Availability & Changes

All products are subject to availability. We reserve the right to change any content or packaging to provide an item of equal or greater value without prior notice.

Our Story

Bliss & Bloom Flower Shop began with a simple idea: that flowers are more than decorations—they are feelings, memories, and messages from the heart.

Founded in 2018 by a small group of friends who shared a love for flowers and a passion for creating meaningful moments, what started as a tiny workspace with a few buckets of fresh blooms slowly grew into a place filled with laughter, creativity, and stories.

In the early days, the team handled everything themselves—from arranging bouquets late at night to personally delivering flowers to customers' doorsteps.

"Bliss" represents the happiness felt when receiving flowers, while "Bloom" reflects growth—not just of flowers, but of relationships, dreams, and community.

Each arrangement was carefully made, inspired by real-life moments such as birthdays, weddings, apologies, celebrations, and even quiet goodbyes.

Over time, the shop became known for its personal touch. Customers didn't just place orders; they shared their stories. The team listened, offered suggestions, and created designs that matched the emotions behind every occasion.

Today, Bliss & Bloom Flower Shop continues to grow while staying true to its roots. The team remains committed to quality, creativity, and heartfelt service. Every bouquet is made with care, ensuring that each flower tells a story—your story.

At Bliss & Bloom, flowers are not just sold; they are shared with love.

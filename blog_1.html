Things to Consider Before Placing Your Order

Posted by Admin · 0 Comments · 3,154 Reads

These guides help you on how to properly place your order in correct details

These guides help you on how to properly place your order with correct details:

  • Shop: (Bliss & Bloom), find your wanted product and order it.
  • Order Info: Finalize your wanted product. Complete the form with provided details (Sender name, recipient, receiver, message, and quantity of the product).
  • Shopping Cart: Provide your details and you can finalize your product (quantity).
  • Checkout: Make sure you've registered and logged in on our site. (If not, you can use the guest checkout).
  • Billing Details: Provide your account & billing details.
  • Delivery Details: Select your delivery date and be aware that we have additional charges between the areas. Visit www.blissandbloom.ph/flowershop-delivery-fee for more information.
  • Delivery Method: Select your delivery date. Please note additional delivery fees may apply depending on area.
  • Payment Method: Then select if you'll pay via card or money transfer.
  • Confirm Order: Finalize your item/s. If you have any complaints or cancelled planned orders, please message us directly at least 2 days before the delivery.
  • Be Aware: You can cancel your orders, but you should communicate with us at least 2 days before the delivery date.
  • Note: If you have any other concerns, kindly contact us.

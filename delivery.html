Delivery Policy

Please review our delivery guidelines

Standard Delivery Fee Areas

Petal Town, Roseville, Sunview — ₱450
Lily Heights, Garden District, Bloomfield — ₱750

Service Coverage

Delivery is available only in selected areas within our service zone. Orders outside this area may be delivered via a third-party courier arranged by our team, with delivery fees added to the total order amount.

Delivery Schedule

Morning: 9:00 AM – 12:00 NN
Afternoon: 1:00 PM – 5:00 PM

Delivery Adjustments

Delivery times may change due to order volume, traffic conditions, or other unforeseen circumstances. All arrangements will be dispatched in their freshest condition. Courier Arrangement For areas within and nearby, customers may book their own courier or request our team to arrange delivery at an additional cost.

Courier Arrangement

Customers within or nearby service areas may book their own courier or request our team to arrange delivery at an additional cost.

Store Pick-Up

Store pick-up is encouraged for customers who wish to receive their orders at their preferred time.

Payment Requirement

Orders must be fully paid before dispatch. Proof of delivery will be provided once the order has been successfully delivered.
